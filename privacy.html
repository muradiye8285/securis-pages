Privacy Policy

Securis Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 29, 2026

Securis respects your privacy. Securis is designed as a local privacy and security audit tool for iPhone and iPad.

Securis does not collect, sell, or share your personal data. Your audit history stays on your device.

View Securis on the App Store

Data Collection

Securis does not collect personal data in the current version. The app does not require an account and does not send audit history to a backend server.

Local Audit History

Securis stores scan history locally on your device so you can review previous audit results. You can clear scan history inside the app settings.

Security Checks

Securis uses available system signals and user-guided checks to provide privacy and security recommendations. Some checks are educational recommendations and may require you to review settings manually.

PDF Reports

If you export a PDF report, the report is generated locally on your device. If you choose to share the PDF through the system share sheet, the destination you select may process the file according to that service's own privacy practices.

Purchases

Securis Pro is handled through Apple's in-app purchase system. Securis does not receive or store your payment card information.

Analytics, Ads, and Tracking

  • No third-party analytics SDKs are included.
  • No advertising SDKs are included.
  • No cross-app tracking is performed.

No Full Device Scan

Securis is a privacy and security audit tool. It is not an antivirus and does not scan the entire device.

Children's Privacy

Securis does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support through the support page: Securis Support.
